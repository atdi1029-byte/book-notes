The equation

total(n) = K · (1 − e−n/τ) n = videos processed · K = 562 (ceiling) · τ = 39 videos · fitted by least squares to the 62 points above

Finance has a big but finite vocabulary, and this channel draws from one bag of it. Picture a bag of K marbles: every video grabs a handful, but only the ones you don't already own count as new. Early on nearly every grab is new; later almost every grab is a repeat. That produces a curve that climbs fast and flattens toward K, and the formula above is the standard shape for it. τ ("tau") is how many videos it takes to burn through about 63% of the bag.

The bot refits K and τ every time it rebuilds this page: it tries every plausible pair and keeps the one whose curve sits closest to the real dots. Everything else falls out of those two numbers. The rate of new concepts at video n is (K/τ)·e−n/τ, which is 2.9 today; it drops below one per video at video 105. At the channel's pace of 1.2 videos a day, that is roughly ~5 wk away.

Caveats: this is one channel's vocabulary fitted with two parameters, so treat it as a trend line, not a promise. When the presenter opens a topic the channel hasn't covered, that video spikes (the dark bars above) and the ceiling moves up a little; each spike should be smaller and further from the last. The tail is mostly deep cuts, so the must-know material will feel finished a week or two before the bot literally goes quiet. When a few weeks pass with nothing new starred or standard, the goal is met — turn on "Hide deep cuts", finish what's left, and go read books.

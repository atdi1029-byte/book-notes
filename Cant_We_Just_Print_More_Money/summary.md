# Can't We Just Print More Money?

**Authors:** Rupal Patel and Jack Meaning
**Published:** 2022 (Cornerstone Press / Penguin)
**Pages:** 315

Two Bank of England economists answer ten deceptively simple questions to explain the entire field of economics — from why your breakfast arrives on time (markets, supply and demand, the invisible hand) to whether governments can just keep printing money (QE, fiscal multipliers, inflation targets). Written with only one equation in 320 pages, the book makes serious economics accessible without dumbing it down. Covers market failures, climate externalities, labour markets, GDP, trade, inflation, the nature of money, banking, financial crises, and monetary/fiscal policy — all through vivid real-world examples like Uber surge pricing during the Sydney siege, the Bra War of 2005, Zimbabwe's $100 trillion banknotes, and Northern Rock's bank run. The authors are careful to present economics not as a set of settled answers but as an evolving toolkit: models are always wrong but sometimes useful, markets often fail, and the field needs diverse voices challenging orthodoxy.

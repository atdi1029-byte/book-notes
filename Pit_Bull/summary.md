# Pit Bull: Lessons from Wall Street's Champion Trader
## Martin "Buzzy" Schwartz (with Dave Morine and Paul Flint)
### Summary

---

## What This Book Is About

*Pit Bull* is the autobiography of Martin "Buzzy" Schwartz, a former securities analyst who transformed himself into one of the most successful independent traders in Wall Street history. After nine years of losing money in markets despite elite credentials (Amherst, Columbia Business School, U.S. Marines), Schwartz developed a systematic plan with his wife Audrey, switched from fundamental to technical analysis, built a $100,000 grubstake, bought a seat on the American Stock Exchange, and went on to win the U.S. Trading Championship multiple times. The book chronicles his journey from the Amex floor trading options in 1979 through his discovery of S&P 500 futures in 1982, the 1987 crash, his foray into hedge fund management, and his eventual return to trading his own money — the only arrangement that truly suited his personality.

## Key Takeaways

1. **The Plan Changes Everything**: Schwartz was a serial loser for nine years until he and Audrey sat down and created a structured plan with specific goals and deadlines. The plan itself — not some new insight — transformed him from loser to winner.

2. **Methodology Over Information**: Switching from fundamental to technical analysis wasn't about which was "right" — it was about finding a methodology that fit his personality. The Magic T Theory, 10-day EMAs, and oscillators gave him an inner resource to draw on under pressure.

3. **Ego Is the Enemy**: "The most important change in my trading career occurred when I learned to divorce my ego from the trade." The market doesn't know you exist. You're playing against yourself.

4. **Honor Thy Stop**: Every trade needs a defined "uncle point" before entry. Most people have half a plan — they know how much profit they'll take but not how much they'll lose.

5. **The Process Creates Confidence**: Nightly chart work, moving average calculations, and preparation aren't just analysis — they're psychological conditioning that creates the confidence to pull the trigger in real time.

6. **Know Your Game**: Schwartz excelled at trading his own money with quick in-and-out scalping. Managing other people's money (Commodities Corp, Sabrina Partners) changed his style, time horizon, and psychology — always for the worse. He closed his fund and went back to solo trading.

7. **Physical and Psychological Health Are Non-Negotiable**: Pericarditis, Audrey's cancer diagnosis, and chronic stress taught him that 8 hours of sleep, exercise, and therapy aren't luxuries — they're essential trading inputs.

8. **Audrey as Edge**: His wife wasn't just emotional support — she was an active trading partner who read his body language, reinforced good decisions, and screamed "Get smaller!" when he lost control. Her absence during the $800K mink coat loss proved she was essential.

9. **The Grubstake Must Be Earned**: Borrowed money creates wrong incentives. Earning $100K through trading proved the methodology worked AND taught money management discipline.

10. **Protect the Family First**: Every crisis response — going for the gold twice, honoring stops, keeping money in safe deposit boxes — was driven by family protection, not profit maximization.

## Thematic Breakdown

### The Making of a Trader (Chapters 1-3)
Origins story: childhood gambling, college, Marine Corps, securities analyst career. The Plan with Audrey. Grubstake accumulation. First trades on the Amex floor.

### Finding the Edge (Chapters 4-6)
Great Pyramid debacle and unemployment. Discovery of the Magic T Theory. Transformation from fundamentalist to technician. ASA gold options. S&P 500 futures as "the ultimate contract." Cash bond/S&P correlation discovery.

### The Champion (Chapters 7-9)
$800K mink coat loss. U.S. Trading Championships. Sunspot theory. Losing streak management. Park Avenue co-op. Third LaSalle clearing firm crisis.

### The Peak and Beyond (Chapters 10-13)
Art collecting (American Impressionists). 1987 crash survival. Going for the gold. Commodities Corp. Sabrina Partners hedge fund launch.

### The Reckoning (Chapters 14-16)
OPM stress. Investor relations nightmare. Pericarditis. Audrey's cancer. Move to Florida. Therapist. Learning to separate trading from life.

### The Methodology (Chapter 17 + Guide)
Complete trading system: 10-day EMA, channel lines, TICK/TRIN/TIKI, laminated checklist, 5×8 index cards, daily routine from 6:45 AM to 10:30 PM.

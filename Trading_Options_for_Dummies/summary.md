# Trading Options for Dummies (3rd Edition)
**Joe Duarte, MD** | 494 pages | Wiley

A comprehensive introduction to options trading that covers the full spectrum from basic contract mechanics to advanced multi-leg strategies. Duarte, a physician-turned-trader, emphasizes risk management above all — his core philosophy is that if you manage risk first, profits follow. The book walks through call and put fundamentals, the Greeks (delta, gamma, theta, vega), option pricing via Black-Scholes, and how to read an options chain. It then builds through increasingly complex strategies: vertical spreads, straddles, strangles, butterflies, condors, calendar spreads, and ratio spreads. Technical analysis is woven throughout, with chapters on market breadth indicators, sector analysis, and designing trading systems. The practical emphasis on paper trading, position sizing, and having a written trading plan before deploying capital sets it apart from more theory-heavy options texts.

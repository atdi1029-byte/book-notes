### BANK OF ENGLAND TRADE (1992)

[MUST KNOW] **Setup:**
- UK had floating-rate mortgages where rate increases immediately affected homeowners
- Britain joined exchange rate mechanism, requiring currency stability vs Deutsche Mark within set bands

[MUST KNOW] **Strategy:**
- Raising rates to defend currency would bankrupt British homeowners — unsustainable policy
- Band structure created asymmetric risk: push pound one direction while Bank of England could only push back, losing only 2.5%

[MUST KNOW] **Execution — the Soros moment:**
- Druckenmiller proposed using 100% of the fund
- "Soros gave me this really sour look"
- Soros replied: "Why wouldn't you do three times that deal?"
- September 1992: profited ~20% in a single day

[MUST KNOW] **The move after the move:**
- Druckenmiller's follow-up insight: "There's the move after the move"

- Key lesson: don't just capture the event, capture the aftermath
